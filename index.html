How it works

From link to living desktop in one minute

Three steps the first time. One click every time after.

1

Paste a video link

YouTube, a direct video file, and most video sites work. Short loops (10s–2min) make the best wallpapers.

Your video is fetched by your PC, straight from the source — it never passes through our servers.
2

Install once — 60 seconds

The first click offers the Driftpaper app. It installs to your user folder: no admin password, no system changes, clean uninstall from Windows Settings.

Your browser will ask “Open Driftpaper?” — that's Windows handing your link to the app. Tick “always allow” and it never asks again.
3

Your desktop comes alive

The video plays muted, looping, behind your icons. Change it anytime by pasting a new link — one click, instant.

Driftpaper lives in your system tray: pause, scaling options, run-on-startup — all one right-click away.

FAQ

Fair questions, straight answers

Is Driftpaper safe?
Yes — and you don't have to take our word for it: the app installs only to your own user folder (it literally cannot touch system files — it never asks for admin rights), makes no network requests except downloading the videos you ask for and checking for updates, and the site + releases are public on GitHub. Uninstalling removes every trace.
Why does Windows SmartScreen warn me during install?
Because the beta isn't code-signed yet. Signing certificates identify the publisher to Windows and cost real money — that's planned before the public release. Until then SmartScreen shows “unknown publisher” for any unsigned app, ours included. Choose “More info → Run anyway” if you trust us.
Will it slow down my games or my PC?
No. Driftpaper pauses automatically whenever a fullscreen app is in front — games get 100% of your GPU. Decoding uses your graphics card's video engine (the same hardware that plays YouTube), so normal desktop use barely notices it. It also pauses when nothing is visible and, on laptops, when you're on battery.
Where do my videos go? Do you see them?
Videos are downloaded by your PC, directly from the source site, into your local app-data folder — they never pass through any Driftpaper server (we don't have servers). Nothing about your usage is collected or sent anywhere.
Does sound play?
No — wallpapers are always muted. That also lets Driftpaper download a video-only stream, which is faster and lighter.
How do I uninstall?
Windows Settings → Apps → Driftpaper → Uninstall. It stops playback, detaches from your desktop, and removes the app, its cache, and its settings — verified to leave zero traces.
